    Questions

    Good to know.

    How far ahead should we book?

    For tents and dance floors, 3 to 6 months ahead, especially for weekends in the dry season. Chairs and linens can often be booked a few weeks out.

    Do you deliver to Tobago?

    Yes. Tobago deliveries travel on the cargo ferry, so we confirm dates early and add a flat ferry and crew charge to your quote.

    What happens if it rains?

    Our sailcloth tents are rated for tropical showers and come with optional side walls. We'll recommend a rain plan at your site visit.

    Is the damage deposit refundable?

    Yes. It is returned in full within 7 days of collection once everything is checked back in.
